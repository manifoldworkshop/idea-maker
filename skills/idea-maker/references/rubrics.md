# Idea quality rubric

## Clarity
- The idea is understandable in under a minute.
- The problem and user are explicit.

## Feasibility
- Execution steps are realistic for the stated scope.
- Major dependencies or constraints are surfaced.

## Differentiation
- The idea is not just a generic clone.
- A clear angle or insight is stated.

## Measurability
- Success metrics are plausible and tied to outcomes.

## Brevity
- The idea is concise; only expand when needed.
