# IDEA.md schema

## Frontmatter (optional)

```
---
name: <idea name>
status: draft | exploring | validated | building | shipped
owner: <name or team>
last_updated: <YYYY-MM-DD>
tags: ["tag1", "tag2"]
complexity: low | medium | high
impact: low | medium | high
---
```

## Body sections (required unless marked optional)

- Summary (required): 2-6 sentences describing the idea.
- Motivation / Opportunity (optional): what draws you to this idea and why it matters.
- Participants / Beneficiaries (optional): who benefits or participates and why.
- Approach (optional): how the idea could be expressed at a high level.
- What Makes It Special (optional): why it is meaningfully distinct or delightful.
- Next Steps (optional): high-level steps or phases.
- Risks & Unknowns (optional): open questions and dependencies.
- Success Signals (optional): how to notice progress or success.
- References (optional): links to supporting docs in `references/`.

## Style

- Use clear headings and short paragraphs.
- Prefer concrete outcomes over vague ambition.
- Avoid long lists unless necessary.
